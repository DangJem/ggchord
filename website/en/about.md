## Data and artwork {#sources}

The [data and asset credits](../ASSET-SOURCES.md) identify the sources of the sequence records, annotation catalogues and ggchord artwork. For a particular figure, open its [case](gallery.md) to find the inputs and the plotting code.

<a id="feedback"></a>

## Report a problem {#feedback}

Include the smallest input that reproduces the problem, the R code, and the output of `packageVersion("ggchord")`. For a drawing issue, attach the figure and its intended width and height. [Report an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/DangJem/ggchord/issues)
